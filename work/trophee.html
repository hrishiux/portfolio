Case study 06

Trophee — Manga Reader & WebApp

Freelance design work for Trophee.xyz, a Singapore-based manga NFT platform, from 2021 to 2022. Sole designer on their Manga Reader — initially scoped as a mobile app, later shipped as a webapp at manga.trophee.xyz.

Role
UI/UX Designer (Freelance)
Timeline
2021–2022
Client
Trophee.xyz, Singapore
Scope
Sole designer, end to end

About Trophee

Trophee sat at the intersection of manga and NFTs, working with established manga artists such as Goseki Kojima (Lone Wolf and Cub) and Yuji Kaida (known for Godzilla concept art). The platform ran on Binance Smart Chain and used its own token, $MANGA, for purchases and staking, with the goal of giving manga creators a new revenue channel and collectors a way to own verifiable digital manga art.

Role & responsibilities

As lead and sole designer on the Manga Reader, I owned the product end to end:

  • Defined the visual identity for the reader and webapp
  • Designed wireframes, prototypes, and final high-fidelity screens for both mobile and web
  • Covered the complete flow — library browsing, the reader itself, login, and the coin-based purchase system
  • Designed with technical constraints in mind (NFT/coin-gated content) alongside usability and visual polish
  • Delivered final screens ready for handoff to development

What I built

The scope covered the entire user journey: browsing the manga library, reading, logging in, and purchasing through Trophee’s coin system tied to NFT ownership. The main design challenge was making an NFT-gated content model feel like a normal manga-reading experience rather than a marketplace bolted onto a reader — so ownership and access needed to stay in the background while reading and browsing stayed frictionless.

Outcome

The alpha version of the Trophee Manga Reader launched at manga.trophee.xyz.
