E20 and AFR (engine air–fuel mixture)

In seven years the ethanol in Indian petrol went from 5% to 20%. Ethanol burns with less air, so every engine’s target mixture moved. This is what that does to an engine, and to the three I own.

01

The target moved

Engines don’t aim for a fixed air–fuel ratio. They aim for λ 1, exactly enough air to burn the fuel. Ethanol carries oxygen of its own, so the more of it in the tank, the less air the engine needs.

02

An engine that doesn’t adapt runs leaner

While cruising, an oxygen sensor lets the engine computer correct for the new fuel. At full throttle many engines stop listening and run a fixed, rich recipe. A US lab caught this on a 2009 Honda Civic 1.8: the same engine and the same test, on two fuels.

03

It isn’t just one car

The US Department of Energy ran sixteen cars at full throttle on E0 and E20. Nine kept their mixture. Seven ran leaner, and their catalytic converters ran hotter for it.

04

So how much mileage do you lose?

Every source agrees E20 gives fewer kilometres per litre. They disagree on how many, partly because they compare it with different fuels. Physics gives a reference: a litre of E20 holds about 7% less energy than petrol, and about 3.8% less than E10.

05

My three on E20

No public mixture logs exist for my exact engines. Here is what can honestly be said about each.

Turbo · direct injection

Octavia Mk2 1.8 TSI

λ 1

Audi’s stated target for the EA888, from its first-generation manual

0.98–1.02

λ of a stock third-generation EA888 at full throttle, up to 4,300 rpm

Closed-loop control is where an engine absorbs E20’s extra oxygen. My own VCDS scans, taken while the car ran E20, hold no lean, rich or fuel-trim faults.

Non-turbo · port injection

Suzuki SY416 (Baleno)

1995

Year its G16B engine went into the Baleno

Designed before India sold any blend. Whether it carries fuel trim into full throttle, as the Civic doesn’t, is undocumented. ARAI found some older rubber parts may age faster on E20.

Single cylinder · fuel injection

Honda CBR250R

3–4%

Economy drop NITI Aayog gives two-wheelers

On a dyno the stock bike already runs about λ 0.95 (14 : 1) at full throttle, leaner than the usual band. How it behaves on E20 hasn’t been measured in public.

Part two

The same data, as a working tool

A story answers one question. The people who run engines, test cells or production lines need to ask their own. These tools take the data behind the story and hand the questions to the reader: watch a sensor feed, query any dataset, trace any number to where it came from.

06 · Monitor

Watch a sensor feed

Six minutes of a stock VW 1.6 logged from its engine computer: 185 samples of λ, engine speed, load and injection time. Each sample is classed by a rule you can read. Drag across the strip to select any stretch; the statistics and histogram follow.

07 · Explorer

Ask your own question

Eight datasets from the study, one scatter. Pick any two fields and a filter. The query line states exactly what is plotted, and the correlation is computed on the points you see.

08 · Lineage

Trace every number

A chart is only as trustworthy as the trail behind it. Every source on the left, grouped by the kind of evidence, linked to every chart on this page it feeds. Hover either side to trace the links; click to jump.

09 · Handoff

Specified for the engineers who build it

Accessibility

  • Every chart has a table with the same numbers.
  • Marks with tooltips take keyboard focus; the monitor’s selections have buttons as well as dragging.
  • Fuels are labelled directly on each chart, not told apart by colour alone. Measured and stated figures differ by fill, not hue.
  • Dark mode is its own set of tokens, not an inversion. The opening animation stops when the system asks for reduced motion.

Tokens

Read live from the stylesheet, so they change with the theme. Type: Archivo for headings and controls, EB Garamond for reading, JetBrains Mono for data.
