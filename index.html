The Human

Designer who builds
things with his hands.

I'm Hrishikesh. Studied design at IIT Guwahati, then joined Upstox as lead designer on their account-opening product — the first experience every new user goes through. Real stakes, real conversion numbers, real engineers to argue with.

Outside screens: I co-founded an anime app, spent 2025 doing ECU calibration at an IIT Madras automotive startup, and yes, I know what it feels like to have an engine apart on the garage floor and not entirely know how it goes back together.

Great design and mechanical intuition come from the same place — understanding how things actually work well enough to make them feel effortless.
