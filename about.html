off the clock

weekends in the garage

Didn’t drive a car until my mid-twenties. Rode two-wheelers only for the first several years of adult life, never got around to learning. June 2023 I put a month into it, full focus, and picked it up fast. Somewhere in that month it turned into wanting to understand the mechanical side of things properly, not just drive.

What followed was a run of buying, fixing, and sometimes selling cars and bikes.

2023 · sold

MS800

A relative’s car that had been sitting abandoned. Got it running again, drove it for a couple of months, sold it running clean.

first car · sold

Honda City

First car I bought, after months of looking. Had suspension issues — worn out, needed full replacement including ball joints — which at the time I didn’t have the tools or setup to handle. Fixed the engine side of things, drove it, eventually sold it. Still on the list to buy back someday.

still own it

Suzuki SY416

Still own it, most-worked-on car of the lot. Engine mounts, head gasket, intake, and exhaust done myself; clutch done with help from a shop. Full interior redo too — cluster, audio system, speakers, gear knob. Car’s 17–20 years old and no previous owner kept it in great shape, so the goal throughout has been OE-plus across the board.

MVD auction · runs now

Honda CBR250R

Bought at a state MVD auction — a bike that had been seized and sat in a shed for about seven years. Looked decent physically, mechanically dead.

Set one rule going in: don’t buy a replacement part if it can be fixed instead. Partly budget, partly the point — swapping every broken part on a bike like this until it runs ends up costing more than a well-maintained used one anyway, and CBR250R parts aren’t easy to find in India on top of that. The injector was clogged solid with old fuel gunk; built a rig to clean it instead of buying a new one. Took about three months, done in spare time. Runs now.

2011 · learning on it

Skoda Laura

Bought for scrap value, shipped down from Delhi (HR plate), paperwork cleared. Using it to learn turbo tuning in general — boost pressure, air-fuel mixture, boost variables, that side of things.
