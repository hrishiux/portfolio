off the clock

weekends in the garage

Didn’t drive a car until my mid-twenties. Rode two-wheelers only for the first several years of adult life, never got around to learning. June 2023 I put a month into it, full focus, and picked it up fast. Somewhere in that month it turned into wanting to understand the mechanical side of things properly, not just drive. What followed was a run of buying, fixing, and sometimes selling cars and bikes.

  • MS800

    A relative’s car that had been sitting abandoned. Got it running again, drove it for a couple of months, sold it running clean.

  • Honda City

    First car I bought, after months of looking. Had suspension issues — worn out, needed full replacement including ball joints — which at the time I didn’t have the tools or setup to handle. Fixed the engine side of things, drove it, eventually sold it. Still on the list to buy back someday.

  • Suzuki SY416

    Still own it, most-worked-on car of the lot. Engine mounts, head gasket, intake, and exhaust done myself; clutch done with help from a shop. Full interior redo too — cluster, audio system, speakers, gear knob. Car’s 17–20 years old and no previous owner kept it in great shape, so the goal throughout has been OE-plus across the board.

  • Honda CBR250R

    Bought at a state MVD auction — a bike that had been seized and sat in a shed for about seven years. Looked decent physically, mechanically dead. Set one rule going in: don’t buy a replacement part if it can be fixed instead. The injector was clogged solid with old fuel gunk; built a rig to clean it instead of buying a new one. Took about three months, done in spare time. Runs now.

  • Skoda Laura (2011)

    Bought for scrap value, shipped down from Delhi (HR plate), paperwork cleared. Using it to learn turbo tuning in general — boost pressure, air-fuel mixture, boost variables, that side of things.

  • Special mentions

    Volkswagen Ameo — bought for my dad in 2022, worked on it a bit. Ford EcoSport — replaced the Ameo in April 2025, worked on this one a little too.
